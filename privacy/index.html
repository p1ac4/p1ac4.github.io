Privacy

What this site measures.

A short description of the analytics used across this product site.

Google Analytics

This static site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand page views, traffic sources, scrolling, file downloads, and outbound link interactions. The goal is to learn whether people can discover the products and reach the relevant downloads or documentation.

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and receive information including the page URL, browser and device information, approximate location, and interaction events. Data is processed by Google under its Privacy Policy.

What is not collected intentionally

This site has no account system, forms, advertising personalization, or configured user IDs. Do not place credentials, prompts, API keys, or other sensitive information in page URLs.

Your controls

You can restrict or clear cookies through your browser. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
